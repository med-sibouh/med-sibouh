### About me

- 💳 **Payments.** About 5 years in customer-facing technical roles, 3.5 of them in payments. I onboard global PSPs and enterprise merchants end to end, from kick-off to the first live transaction, across EMEA, APAC and LATAM.
- 🔍 **Technical support.** I investigate payment failures with logs and SQL, isolate whether it's configuration, integration or platform, and hand engineering a clear, reproducible case.
- 🤖 **AI automation.** I build AI tools with Claude and Gemini that turn hours of manual checks into minutes, with a human making the final call.
- 🎓 **Background.** MSc in Computer Science Engineering (Óbuda University, Budapest).
